Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Who I am

CLIPS (“the App”) is a personal tool operated by Aniol. Contact: 72lilk72@gmail.com.

2. What data the App uses

  • TikTok authorization data: when the account owner signs in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and a refresh token. They are used only to upload videos to that same TikTok account.
  • Twitch authorization data: an access token used to create clips through the Twitch API.
  • Public Twitch chat activity: the App counts how many messages arrive over time to detect busy moments. It does not store the content of messages or the names of chat users.
  • Video clips: clips created from public Twitch streams are stored temporarily on the operator’s own computer.

3. How the data is used

Data is used only to create clips and to upload them to the account owner’s TikTok account. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

4. Sharing and storage

I do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties, other than sending uploads to TikTok and Twitch through their APIs as described above. Tokens are stored locally on the operator’s device and are not published.

5. Retention and deletion

The account owner can revoke the App’s access at any time from the TikTok and Twitch security or connected-apps settings. Local tokens and clips can be deleted at any time. To request deletion of any data related to you, write to 72lilk72@gmail.com.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

I may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
